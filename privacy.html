Name Wheel privacy policy

Name Wheel is an internal Google Meet add-on for Edge (edge.app) team calls.

What it reads

The add-on reads the meeting id from the Google Meet add-ons SDK. It does not read your name, email, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, or any Google account data.

What it sends

To keep the wheel in sync across the call, the add-on sends the list of selected names (from a fixed team roster) and the random seed of each spin to an ntfy.sh topic. The topic name is a hash of the meeting id, so the meeting id itself is not sent. No personal data leaves your browser.

What it stores

Nothing is stored on a server we run. ntfy.sh keeps recent messages on a topic for a short time under its own policy.
